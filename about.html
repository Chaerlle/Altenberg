The Pursuit of Uncompromising Precision

Founded on the belief that a luxury automobile should be as mechanically honest as a fine mechanical timepiece, Altenberg engineers vehicles that bridge raw performance and timeless restraint. Every component exists by mathematical necessity.

Chronology of Pursuit

Five Pivotal Epochs — 1968 to Present

1968 01 / 05

The First Chassis

Master engineer Klaus von Altenberg hand-builds the Altenberg 2800 GT in Munich, establishing our reputation for peerless mechanical balance and understated coachwork.

Munich Workshop
1986 02 / 05

Motorsport Dominance

The Altenberg Monza Group A claims consecutive victories in the European Touring Championship, permanently forging our performance engineering pedigree under sustained extreme stress.

Nürburgring • Monza

Monocoque Revolution

Deployment of our proprietary full-aluminum spaceframe chassis architecture, reducing gross curb weight by 22% while simultaneously doubling torsional rigidity parameters.

Patent EP1428741
2018 04 / 05

The Electric Transition

Unveiling of the dedicated 800V E-Platform architecture in Dingolfing, proving beyond dispute that zero local emissions can surpass combustion systems in sheer visceral feedback.

Electrification Matrix
2025 Active Era

Autonomous Precision

Commercial release of Level 3 highway piloted driving systems and verification of net-zero carbon operations across all Bavarian and continental manufacturing sites.
